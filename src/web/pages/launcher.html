Hyperbolic Tiling
Billiards

Euclidean negative-refraction billiards on the image of the ordinary square tiling under a radial map to the open unit disk. Despite the project name, the dynamics use no hyperbolic metric and no hyperbolic geodesics.

The three pages share one server-side run. If popups are blocked, use the individual links above. The diagnostics page is the authoritative control interface.
